# OnPrez First Launch Niche

**Action-plan item:** P2-003 — Define the First Niche Clearly  
**Decision date:** 31 July 2026  
**Launch niche:** Independent beauty and wellness professionals and small teams

## Decision

OnPrez will launch and run its first structured real-user tests with appointment-led beauty and wellness businesses.

The initial niche includes:

- hair stylists and salons;
- makeup artists and studios;
- nail technicians and nail salons;
- massage therapists and massage businesses;
- spas and wellness studios;
- barbers and barber shops;
- mobile beauty and wellness professionals;
- small multi-practitioner teams operating in these categories.

This is an acquisition and product-learning focus, not a platform restriction. Existing support for fitness, tutoring, photography, consulting, home services, and other service categories remains available, but launch messaging, outreach, templates, and structured onboarding research lead with beauty and wellness.

## Why this niche first

Beauty and wellness is a strong match for the first sellable OnPrez loop because these businesses commonly need all of the following at the same time:

1. A visually credible brand page that looks better than a bare booking link.
2. A simple, browsable menu of named services, prices, durations, and optional images.
3. Availability and appointment booking without repeated direct-message exchanges.
4. One memorable link for Instagram, WhatsApp, Google Business Profile, QR codes, and referrals.
5. Mobile-first management because many operators work away from a desk.
6. Support for both solo professionals and small teams.

The existing realistic demos already provide useful niche-specific evidence:

- **Heavenly Pamper Palace** represents massage, spa, and wellness services.
- **Crown & Canvas Studio** represents hair and makeup services.
- The **Regent Barber** template extends the visual range to barbering.

## Primary target user

The first target user is an independent beauty or wellness professional, or the owner of a small team, who:

- receives enquiries or bookings through Instagram, WhatsApp, phone calls, or referrals;
- needs clients to understand services and prices before contacting them;
- benefits from presenting photos, policies, testimonials, and brand details together;
- manages recurring appointment slots;
- does not want to build and maintain a traditional website plus a separate booking product;
- wants a page polished enough to share without being prompted.

## Launch problem statement

> Beauty and wellness professionals often split their brand, service menu, availability, and booking process across social profiles, messages, image posts, and separate booking links. OnPrez gives them one branded presence where clients can understand the business and book.

## Launch value proposition

> Your beauty business, services, prices, availability, and bookings—together at one memorable OnPrez handle.

## Homepage and onboarding message

The homepage and signup metadata lead with beauty and wellness while preserving the wider category selector. The first-screen message must communicate all three product outcomes:

- a branded presence;
- visible services and availability;
- client bookings.

The message should never imply that OnPrez is only a generic scheduling tool. The website-like branding page remains part of the core promise.

## Demo and seed-content standard

A niche demo is acceptable only when it includes:

- a plausible business name, tagline, location, and opening pattern;
- realistic service names, prices, and durations;
- useful policies and trust information;
- imagery or visual treatment suited to the business;
- a complete path from service discovery to booking;
- a clear fiction/demo label where claims are not real.

Heavenly Pamper Palace and Crown & Canvas Studio are the canonical first-niche demos. New seed content should extend, not replace, these examples unless product evidence shows a clearer direction.

## Real-user onboarding protocol

Run the following session with **3–5 beauty and wellness professionals**, including at least one multi-member business.

### Participant mix

Aim for a useful spread across:

- hair or makeup;
- nails, massage, spa, or wellness;
- solo and team-operated businesses;
- people currently booking mainly through direct messages and people already using a booking tool.

### Session rules

1. Use a fresh account on a mobile device.
2. Do not explain the product before the participant reads the homepage.
3. Ask the participant to say what they think OnPrez does and who it is for.
4. Ask them to claim a handle, add or review services, set availability, publish, and share the page.
5. For a team business, include inviting and accepting one team member.
6. Create a test customer booking and ask the business user to manage it.
7. Record task completion, time, assistance required, confusion, trust concerns, and whether they would share the page.
8. Do not record customer personal data in this document.

### Five-second comprehension questions

- What do you think OnPrez is?
- Does this look intended for a business like yours?
- What would a client be able to do here?
- Is this mainly a website, a booking tool, or both?

### Success criteria

P2-003 validation succeeds when:

- at least 3 participants immediately identify beauty or wellness businesses as the intended first audience;
- participants understand that OnPrez combines a branded presence with services, availability, and booking;
- each participant can identify a realistic way they would share the link;
- the demo feels like a plausible professional business rather than generic SaaS filler;
- recurring confusion is converted into a documented copy, template, or onboarding change.

## Scope boundary

Niche focus does not defer or remove team members. Team invitations, secure acceptance, roles, member management, and tenant-safe permissions remain Core under `MVP_SCOPE.md`.

The first-niche decision should be reviewed after either:

- five beauty and wellness businesses have completed the first sellable loop and at least three are actively receiving bookings; or
- three independent participants show the same material mismatch between this niche and the product.
